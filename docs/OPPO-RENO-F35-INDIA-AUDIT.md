## Evidence map

- Primary India specification pages supply dimensions, colour list, memory records, screen, chips, cameras/video, charging, biometrics, radios, software, country and box contents. All source URLs are in each private reference JSON.
- India overview pages supply frame material where explicitly stated, IP ratings with laboratory qualifiers and photo zoom limits. Reno16 has an aluminium frame; F35 Pro has an AM04 aluminium-alloy middle frame. Reno16c frame material remains empty.
- Reno16 and Reno16c India details pages supply 2 July 2026 India release date, 12-month handset warranty, explicit 3.5mm-jack absence and the India range. The Reno16 comparison explicitly says Reno16c has no infrared remote; its own spec page omission alone would not prove No. The comparison source is listed on both records.
- India specs explicitly state NFC and eSIM unsupported on all three models. Country of Origin India describes manufacturing, not brand origin. Brand origin stays empty.
- Periscope architecture is not established. The fixed periscope field remains empty; optical-zoom information identifies the confirmed 50MP telephoto without assigning a periscope design.
- Photo zoom and video zoom are labelled separately: Reno16 overview up to 120× versus video up to 18×; Reno16c up to 60× versus video up to 10×. F35 Pro source only establishes front-video zoom, which stays labelled as such.
- Android base version, update duration, battery chemistry and wireless charging are not inferred from ColorOS numbering or missing bullets. Unsettled values stay empty. Manufacturer software may differ from the installed release.
- Memory records are exact listed pairs. Colour lists do not prove every RAM/storage/colour combination; variant colour and image remain empty. No shop stock, price, review, offer or accessory warranty is invented.
