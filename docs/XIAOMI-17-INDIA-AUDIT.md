- Ultra FAQ says 16 GB + 1 TB, but its specifications and current India store agree on 16 GB + 512 GB. Only the corroborated 512 GB option is retained. The old guide's unconfirmed 256 GB and 1 TB preferences are removed.
- Ultra FAQ says IP68 + 69; specifications describe only the IP68 laboratory test. Only the common IP68 claim is retained, with its freshwater/depth/time qualification. No unrestricted waterproof claim is made.
- Xiaomi 17 lists a charger under package contents but also asks buyers to confirm local adapter inclusion. Ultra's package list omits a charger and says contents vary by region. Both charger-in-box fields remain empty; omission is not a verified No.
- CPU core count 8 is derived from the official two prime plus six performance cores. Xiaomi 17's 60 mm floating telephoto is labelled as such, without inventing a periscope construction or numeric optical-zoom ratio. Ultra's optical-zoom field preserves the stated 75–100 mm focal range.
- eSIM is explicitly named on these two India specification pages; carrier/region conditions remain in its value. This differs from the unconfirmed 17T field.
- Wi-Fi remains “Wi-Fi 7 Ready” with regional/network conditions. Slow-motion rates are not ordinary video frame rates. Model update commitments remain empty in this India audit; search-indexed global policy was not treated as an India launch date or proof of locally applicable support.
