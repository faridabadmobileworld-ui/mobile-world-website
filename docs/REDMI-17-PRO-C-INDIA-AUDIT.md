## Decisions and inconsistencies

- Note 17 Pro has UFS **2.2 for 128GB** and **3.1 for 256GB**. The single storage-version field explicitly associates each version with its storage capacity. Its two actual RAM/storage records remain distinct.
- Note 17 Pro's specification page uses 17.34 cm, while the FAQ says 6.83 inches and overview says 17.35 cm (6.83 inches). The guide/reference uses the corroborated inch value without silently choosing a centimetre conversion. The specification teaser says 3+ days while the overview describes 3.5+ days under Xiaomi's light-use laboratory model; only the latter qualified claim is retained, not a guaranteed endurance figure.
- REDMI 17C overview's bottom paragraph says FHD+, contradicting its exact 1600×720 specification and HD+ FAQ. Use **HD+ / 1600×720**. Do not copy the marketing typo.
- REDMI 17 provides HD+ but no exact pixel dimensions in the checked sources. Its resolution field remains HD+; no 1600×720 value is borrowed from the 17C. Neither 17 nor 17C source explicitly settles panel technology, so LCD/IPS/OLED is not guessed. HBM brightness is labelled HBM rather than represented as a different peak-brightness mode.
- REDMI 17 has a hybrid SIM/microSD slot; 17C is described as Dual SIM + microSD. Their wording is kept separate. For 17/17C only Wi-Fi frequency bands are verified, not a numbered Wi-Fi generation.
- REDMI 17C SA and NSA bands differ: both lists are retained separately. Memory extension is not counted as physical RAM. An auxiliary rear lens is not relabelled as a verified ultrawide or telephoto.
- REDMI 17's protective case is absent from the checked box list, so inclusion is empty rather than No. In-box chargers and cables are explicitly listed. No battery chemistry is inferred for 17/17C. Display glass does not establish the whole front/back/frame composition, and a colour's leather-like texture is not treated as confirmed back material.
- Update durations are years, not Android generations. Marketing about 48-month smoothness or battery-cycle durability is not update support or warranty. A partial-charge time is not substituted for a full-charge time; charging time remains empty for all three. Water-resistance ratings retain manufacturer/laboratory qualifications.
- Existing REDMI 17C sale note, processing-fee offer, availability flag and internal offer-blog destination are preserved. This audit changes technical guide data and images, not store offer terms or stock. Existing artwork and other image files remain in Git.
