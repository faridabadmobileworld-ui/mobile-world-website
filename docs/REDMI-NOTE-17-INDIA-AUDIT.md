## Conservative decisions

- Note 17's specifications use 17.75 cm; its FAQ says 6.9 inches. These are not exact equivalents. The reference and guide retain the specification page's centimetre value without inventing an exact inch conversion.
- Note 17 lists n8 with an unexplained asterisk. The reference keeps the other explicitly listed SA/NSA bands; n8 is excluded pending clarification. This is a verified subset, not a claim that no other band is supported.
- Pro Max thickness is 8.57 mm for a finish called “white” by the source and 8.65 mm for other finishes. The named India colour list uses Secret Sunrise, Stealth Black and Matcha Green. Both measurements are retained as finish-dependent; “white” is not invented as a sellable India colour.
- Fiberglass back is explicitly limited to Secret Sunrise; it is not applied to the Black image or every variant. Frame/front materials remain blank rather than being inferred from protection or promotional appearance.
- Four years of OS updates is preserved as years, not silently converted to four Android generations. Six years of battery-reliability marketing is not a six-year battery warranty.
- Three/four-day battery figures are labelled manufacturer light-use claims. The 70-minute Pro Max full-charge figure is a laboratory claim with conditions, not a guaranteed customer charging time. IP labels do not promise unrestricted waterproofing.
- Both India pages explicitly list included chargers and accessories. Their regional/package qualifications still apply. Wireless charging, NFC, eSIM, GPS, face unlock, gyro, FM and other unconfirmed capabilities are genuinely blank; no capability is recorded as No just because the page omits it.
- In-sensor zoom marketing is not mapped to optical lens zoom. Normal frame rates and slow motion remain separate; unverified rear auxiliary-camera details are not guessed.
